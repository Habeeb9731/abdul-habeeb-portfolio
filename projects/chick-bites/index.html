Overview

A local café needed to be found online. And look as good as the food.

Chick Bites is a café in Surathkal serving shawarmas and burgers. The goal was a warm, appetising website that shows the menu, tells the café’s story and makes it easy to book a table — especially from a phone.

Challenge & solution

What made it hard, and how I approached it.

The challenge

Small business, big first impression

  • Most customers discover the café on their phones.
  • The menu changes, so it had to be easy to update.
  • Reservations needed a simple, friendly flow.
The solution

Warm design, simple structure

  • A food-led visual style with large photography and a handwritten logo.
  • Clear menu pages and a straightforward reservation form.
  • Responsive layouts so the site works just as well on mobile.
Key features

Built around what people actually need.

Menu

Menu pages that put the dishes front and centre.

Reservations

A simple table booking form — party size, date and time.

Mobile-friendly

Built to work smoothly on phones, where most visitors browse.

Responsive

Phone view

The hero and menu adapt to small screens so customers can browse and book on the go.

  • Reservation form built for touch
  • Fast loading on mobile networks
