Overview

Therapy starts with trust. The website had to feel calm, clear and welcoming.

Ecliniqs offers online speech therapy for children and adults, plus family counselling. Families often arrive worried and unsure where to start, so the site focuses on reassurance: clear services, friendly imagery and an easy way to book a session.

Challenge & solution

What made it hard, and how I approached it.

The challenge

A sensitive, unfamiliar service

  • Many families have never tried online therapy before.
  • Different services for children, adults and families.
  • Booking needed to be simple for non-technical users.
The solution

Reassuring design, guided booking

  • Warm photography and plain-language copy that explains how sessions work.
  • Separate service pages for child therapy, adult therapy and family counselling.
  • An appointment page with calendar-based booking and a payment page.

What I learned.
