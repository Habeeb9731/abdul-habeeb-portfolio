Overview

Wedding photography sells on emotion. The website had to get out of the way.

GrandLense is a photography studio covering weddings, portrait sessions and creative shoots. The brief was a portfolio site that feels as cinematic as the work — full-screen imagery, minimal interface, and an easy way for couples to get in touch.

Challenge & solution

What made it hard, and how I approached it.

The challenge

Large images, fast site

  • The site is almost entirely high-resolution photography.
  • Visitors mostly arrive on phones, often from social media.
  • Enquiries needed to reach the studio reliably.
The solution

Image-first, lightweight build

  • A full-screen slider with bold typography and minimal chrome, so each photo gets the whole screen.
  • Responsive layouts and compressed imagery for fast loading on mobile.
  • A simple PHP contact form that sends enquiries straight to the studio.
Key features

Built around what people actually need.

Cinematic slider

Responsive
