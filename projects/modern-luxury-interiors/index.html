Overview

A luxury interior firm needs a website that feels as considered as its spaces.

Modern Luxury is a Dubai-based interior design and fit-out firm. The site presents their services — from architectural design and turnkey projects to landscaping and technical services — with a refined, architectural visual language.

Challenge & solution

What made it hard, and how I approached it.

The challenge

Many services, one premium brand

  • A wide range of services — design, fit-out, landscaping, maintenance.
  • A premium audience that judges quality instantly.
  • Enquiries needed to be quick, including via WhatsApp.
The solution

Architectural design, clear structure

  • Bold typography over architectural photography to set a premium tone.
  • Dedicated pages for each service so clients find exactly what they need.
  • Prominent quote and WhatsApp contact options on every page.
Key features

Built around what people actually need.

Service pages

Separate pages for architecture design, turnkey, landscaping and technical services.

Portfolio

Project portfolio and detail pages to showcase completed work.

Fast contact

“Get a quote” and WhatsApp buttons always within reach.

Responsive

Premium on mobile

The layout keeps the architectural feel on phones, with contact options always one tap away.

  • Architectural hero adapts to portrait
  • Quote and WhatsApp always one tap away
  • Service pages stack cleanly
